What it does

  • Dictation everywhere: hold Ctrl + Win, speak, let go. Double-tap for hands-free.
  • English and Hinglish, decided automatically for each sentence.
  • Clean-up: removes "um" and "uh", fixes "2 PM... actually 3 PM" to "3 PM", formats spoken lists. It never invents words; when unsure it leaves your text alone.
  • Edit by voice: select text, press Alt + Shift + C, say "make this a bulleted list". Word and Google Docs get a real list.
  • Meeting notes: records your microphone and the call audio, then writes a summary with action items.
  • Your words: a dictionary for names and terms (CMA, EBITDA, GST...), snippets, and a history with your original words kept.

Privacy

  • On Windows, speech is recognised on your own computer by default. Your voice does not leave it.
  • Optional AI clean-up and cloud recognition use your own free Groq key, and only after you agree. The app shows a log of everything it sent.
  • No account, no tracking, no ads.

Accuracy

We publish only numbers we measured ourselves, with how they were measured: see the test results. Speech recognition is never perfect; every dictation can be corrected, and your original words are kept.
